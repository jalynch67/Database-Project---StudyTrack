About StudyTrack

StudyTrack is a simple study planner for organising subjects, tasks, deadlines, priorities, and progress in one place.

What you can do

  • Organise subjects Create, update, review, and delete the subjects you study.
  • Plan study tasks Add deadlines and priorities to work linked to each subject.
  • Track progress Move tasks between open and complete as your work progresses.
  • Review your workload Use the dashboard and task filters to focus on upcoming work.
